Privacy Policy — MySize10/10 Certificate Generator

Last updated: [today's date]

What this application does

This automation generates course completion certificates for MySize10/10 students. It accesses Google Drive and Google Sheets to create certificate files and maintain a record of issued certificates, then sends the finished certificate to the student by email.

What data is accessed

  • Google Drive: used to copy a certificate template, edit it with student details, and export the result as a PDF
  • Google Sheets: used to record issued certificates (student name, course, and certificate number) for future lookup, in case a student needs their certificate reissued

What personal data is processed

Student first and last name, course name, and a generated certificate number. This information is provided directly by MySize10/10 staff through a form submission, not collected from the student directly by this tool.

How data is used

Data is used solely to generate the certificate and email it to the correct recipient. It is not shared with third parties, sold, or used for any purpose beyond certificate issuance and record-keeping.

Data storage

Certificate records are stored in a Google Sheet and Google Drive folder owned and controlled by MySize10/10. No data is stored on external servers outside of this Google account.

Retention

Certificate numbers and student names are retained in the Google Sheet indefinitely, solely to allow certificate reissuance if a student loses their original certificate.

Revoking access

The connected Google account owner can revoke this application's access at any time via Google Account settings.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to your-email@example.com.
