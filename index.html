MySize10/10 Certificate Generator

An internal automation built for MySize10/10, a body contouring academy, to generate and email student course completion certificates automatically.

The tool takes student and course details, generates a unique certificate number for record-keeping, creates a certificate from a template, exports it as a PDF, and emails it to the student — replacing manual certificate creation.

This is an internal tool for MySize10/10 and is not a public consumer product.
